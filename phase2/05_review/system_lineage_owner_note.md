## 1. Source Systems Are Inspiration, Not the Final System

InkOS / writing Skill systems / GitHub writing Agent research are important intellectual sources, but they are not Novel Research Lab itself.

They contributed engineering instincts:

- write through durable files and skills, not one-shot prompts
- split reading, transformation, writing, and auditing into separate roles
- preserve Story Bible / World Building / State Ledger records
- support recursive outline growth and review loops
- keep human review gates around risky creative transitions

But Novel Research Lab is an independent system. Its actual implementation path is defined by its own phase structure, schemas, mechanism library, review gates, and Owner decisions.

## 3. Why Phase 2A-2R Is the Right Correction

The first three candidates were valid engineering artifacts, but they exposed a real risk: the system could become too obedient to mechanism recipes and lose the large-scale life force required for long-form web fiction.

Phase 2A-2R fixes that without discarding the work:

- previous candidates become a concept material pool
- Core7 mechanisms remain useful fuel
- schema remains a later convergence tool
- originality review shifts from word panic to structure-level anti-copying
- grand synthesis returns to market-, time-, and reader-validated narrative vitality

## 4. Future Risks To Avoid

The next phases must avoid three collapses:

1. Only engineering process remains, with no creative life.
2. Only mechanism indexes remain, with no market / reader / time validation.
3. Only candidate templates remain, with no grand synthesis engine.

Operational rule:

- Let research and mechanism assets feed imagination.
- Let imagination open the world, fate, conflict, and reader promise.
- Let schemas and ledgers converge and audit the chosen direction afterward.

## 5. Owner-Level Conclusion

Novel Research Lab has not become a clone of InkOS, GitHub writing agents, or any single writing Skill system. It has absorbed their useful engineering ideas and converted them into an independent research-to-creation architecture.

The system remains on track, provided the next creative stage treats "grand synthesis" as a long-form narrative engine rather than a fixed candidate template.
