## Strengths

- **Bible Fidelity**: The nine final assets remain strictly faithful to the approved Story Bible core principles. The mother’s lifespan debt is central to the emotional stakes, the Poverty City mutual aid is manifested in the Mudlamp Lane faction, the protection-array pressure is represented in the ledger integrity, and the physical backlash mechanisms are explicitly defined.
- **Structural Coherence**: The relationships among the nine assets form a completely closed, mutually referenceable network. Every target ID specified in the relationships (e.g., from Lu Chenyuan to Su Wan, Mudlamp Lane, Ashlight Cup, and Embertrace Return) corresponds to an active, validated asset in the bundle.
- **Originality Safeguards**: The originality policies for characters, techniques, and artifacts are robust and comply with the red lines. The naming roots (`["烬", "灯", "痕", "裂", "归", "巷", "骨"]`) are grounded in local, sensory realities, avoiding generic high-fantasy tropes and successfully bypassing all forbidden collisions.
- **Sensory Transmutation**: The system successfully translates abstract financial and auditing concepts into concrete fantasy imagery, such as the粗陶盏 (cracked clay lamp), 灰雨印痕 (grey rain bone marks), and white hair/splitting skin backlash.

## Non-Blocking Improvements

- **Naming Root Expansion**: The current naming roots in `naming_lifefire_local_style` are heavily biased toward the immediate Poverty City/Mudlamp Lane environment. As the protagonist transitions to prefecture or state levels in later arcs, the naming system will require a corresponding set of roots for higher-level administrative, tax, and celestial entities.
- **Sanity Tracking Sync**: The character asset for Lu Chenyuan lists a specific `sanity_level` metric (`0.72`), but this metric is not tracked in the `state_ledger_poorcity_returnfire` tracking metrics. While this allows narrative flexibility, future iterations could explicitly integrate sanity changes into the ledger balance.

## Controlled Prose Test Constraints

- **Visible Physical Cost**: Any prose generation must depict Lu Chenyuan’s physical backlash (white hair growth, splitting skin showing glowing ember lines, coughing up cold ash) immediately upon casting the `烬痕归火术` or activating the `灰烬回灯盏`.
- **Communal Reciprocity**: The Mudlamp Lane mutual-aid network must never be treated as passive fuel for the protagonist. The community must actively protect each other, and Lu Chenyuan’s interventions must be constrained by the moral requirement to protect his neighbors.
- **Sensory Evidence presentation**: Evidence of repetitive taxation or lifespan theft must be presented as physical, supernatural remnants (e.g., grey ash rain that leaves dark red marks on contact with skin) rather than clerical records or spreadsheets.
- **No Schema Jargon**: Modern technical vocabulary and database terms (such as "ledger states," "exposure rate," "transaction boundaries," and "stability metrics") must be completely stripped from the reader-facing text, thoughts, and dialogues. All mechanics must be described through appropriate Chinese fantasy terminology.
