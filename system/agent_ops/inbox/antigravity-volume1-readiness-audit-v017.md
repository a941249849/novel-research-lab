# Antigravity Volume 1 Readiness Audit v0.17

## Verdict

READY_FOR_ASSET_EXPANSION. The approved Story Bible (`borrowed_heaven_life_account_bible.md`) and the Volume 1 Outline Tree (`borrowed_heaven_volume1_outline_tree.md`) provide a structurally sound, highly original, and coherent blueprint for the work. The Controlled Prose Test (`hermes-controlled-prose-test-v016.md`) has been successfully audited and approved, proving that the abstract lifespacing/taxation world engine can be rendered into high-quality, emotionally engaging literary text without database or technical jargon. No blocking defects exist in the core narrative design. However, several critical asset coverage gaps—specifically missing character cards, institutional factions, combat techniques, and transition-level naming assets—must be expanded and promoted from seed status before full production can safely proceed.

## Volume 1 Narrative Spine

The narrative of Volume 1 is structured around a tight, escalatory loop wherein the protagonist Lu Chenyuan (陆沉渊) must alter local life-tax ledger allocations to resolve his mother Su Wan's (苏晚) decaying lifelight and save Mudlamp Lane (泥灯巷) from systematic life-tax duplication. The arc clusters progress as follows:

*   **Arc Cluster 1 (Establishment)**: Low-level clerk Lu Chenyuan discovers the shared origin between his mother’s decaying lifelight and the Mudlamp Lane century-old tax ledger anomalies.
*   **Arc Cluster 2 (Communal Scope)**: The ward array of Mudlamp Lane cracks due to tax pressure, forcing residents to pool lifelight and initiating the search for physical evidence.
*   **Arc Cluster 3 (Action Conflict)**: The arrival of the primary debt collector (初级收债者) forces a direct magical confrontation, exposing the collector's flawed mandate.
*   **Arc Cluster 4 (Public Climax)**: A public trial at the local Life Registry Office (命籍衙命审台), where Lu Chenyuan uses the Ashlight Cup (灰烬回灯盏) to prove tax duplication, causing the balance of the heavenly scales to tip and local life-force to return to the residents.
*   **Arc Cluster 5 (Scale Expansion)**: Lu Chenyuan retrieves evidence from the ruins of the Abandoned Life City (废命地), discovering a regional transport registry seal that links the local error to the Prefecture Life Treasury (州府命库).
*   **Arc Cluster 6 (Resolution & Transition)**: The community refuses to surrender Lu Chenyuan, who executes a local redirection of the tax burden and departs from Mudlamp Lane towards the Prefecture-level boundary to escape upgraded regional pursuit.

## Asset Coverage Gaps

While the nine core assets are active and verified, the Outline Tree relies on several entities and mechanics that currently exist only as unpromoted seed templates or remain entirely undefined in the asset registry:

*   **NPC Gaps**:
    *   *Su Wan (苏晚) Character Card*: Although the emotional bond `bond_mother_su_wan` is active, Su Wan herself lacks a dedicated NPC card defining her state parameters, health decay rate, and interaction thresholds.
    *   *Primary Debt Collector (初级收债者) NPC Asset*: The primary action-phase antagonist of Arc Clusters 3, 4, and 6 lacks a defined combat profile and behavior ledger.
    *   *Minor Registrar / Registry Chief (命籍小吏/主管) NPC Assets*: The local bureaucratic antagonists of the Life Registry Office (命籍衙) are not individually cataloged.
    *   *Prefecture Envoy / Shadow Figure (州府命库影子人物) NPC Asset*: The high-level agent representing the regional treasury in Arc Clusters 5 and 6 is missing.
*   **Faction Gaps**:
    *   *Life Registry Office (命籍衙) Faction Asset*: The primary administrative antagonist of Volume 1 remains a generic seed.
    *   *Prefecture Life Treasury (州府命库) Faction Asset*: The regional power structure that dictates the tax quotas is undefined.
*   **Technique Gaps**:
    *   *Ledger Recognition (识账术) Technique Asset*: The core sensory and investigative technique used by Lu Chenyuan to read lifespans and identify ledger anomalies.
    *   *Chain Deflection / Seal Breaking (反账命印) Technique Asset*: The combat and sealing systems used by the protagonist to intercept and divert collector chains.
*   **Artifact Gaps**:
    *   *Prefecture Transport Registry Seal (州府转运残印) Artifact/Clue Asset*: The physical clue linking Poverty City to the wider systemic conspiracy.
*   **Naming Gaps**:
    *   *Prefecture-Level Naming Style (州府及仙朝命名规范) Naming Asset*: The current naming asset is heavily biased toward Poverty City local style; higher-level administrative, tax, and celestial entities require a distinct set of roots.
*   **Emotional Bond Gaps**:
    *   *Community Trust Bond*: A metric tracking trust variations between Lu Chenyuan and the Mudlamp Lane residents as he exposes them to danger.
*   **Foreshadowing Gaps**:
    *   *Karmic Ledger Heritage (古债契约碎影) Clue Asset*: The fragmented memory/data from ancient covenants exposed during the trial.
*   **State Ledger Gaps**:
    *   *Sanity tracking metrics* (`sanity_level`) are defined in the protagonist's character card but are missing from the `state_ledger_poorcity_returnfire` tracking schema, creating a sync gap.

## Pacing and Originality Risks

*   **Pacing Risks**:
    *   *Dryness & Over-Systemization*: The story could devolve into a bureaucratic audit or mathematical game if tax calculations and ledger mechanics are explained through raw exposition rather than being tied directly to immediate stakes (e.g., the decay of Su Wan's life, the collapse of the ward array).
    *   *Premature Scale Inflation*: Introducing high-level concepts (such as the Celestial Dynasty's core conspiracy or the full details of the domain debt) too early will dilute the claustrophobic, high-stakes tension of Mudlamp Lane's survival.
    *   *Repetitive Action Loops*: Ensure the magical confrontations in Arc Clusters 3, 4, and 6 feel distinct. The collector fights must evolve from desperate survival (Arc 3) to structured legal/magical argument (Arc 4) to escape-oriented redirection (Arc 6).
*   **Originality Risks**:
    *   *Classic Cultivation Tropes (Cliché Clashing)*: High risk of sliding into standard "trash-tier start" or "underdog revenge" formulas (e.g., arrogant young masters, family expulsions). The audit must enforce the "no-cliché" policy, ensuring all conflicts stem strictly from the unique lifespan-taxation world engine.
    *   *Forbidden term and naming collisions*: Must vigilantly guard against the accidental injection of forbidden concepts from the training set (e.g., "至尊骨" (Supreme Bone), "重瞳" (Double Pupils), "掌天瓶" (Heaven-Palm Vial), etc.).

## Controlled Production Recommendation

*   **Scene Approval**: The approved Hermes prose test (`hermes-controlled-prose-test-v016.md`) should serve as the golden style guide.
*   **Production Bounding**: Phase 1 assets (Life Registry Office, Registrar, Ledger Recognition technique) should be promoted immediately under a strict controlled production framework.
*   **Writing Constraints**:
    *   All writers/agents must depict Lu Chenyuan’s physical backlash (white hair growth, splitting skin showing glowing ember lines, coughing up cold ash) immediately upon casting.
    *   The Mudlamp Lane mutual-aid network must remain active and protective; they are not passive fuel for the protagonist.
    *   Evidence of duplicate taxation must be presented as physical, supernatural remnants (e.g., grey ash rain) rather than clerical records or spreadsheets.
    *   No modern database jargon ("exposure rate," "ledger status," etc.) is allowed in the final text.
*   **Execution**: Approve production on a scene-by-scene basis, starting with Chapter 1 (Arc Cluster 1) to test the integration of the newly expanded character assets before launching full-volume serialization.
