## Rejection Reasons

**样本 01 — No rejection flags.**

Every introduced object does causal work. The fire-starter falls on brick, not on the flowing oil — precise physics, not author fiat. Wind backdrafting through the stove vent lifts the hidden page organically; the discovery is mechanical, not convenient. The key dislodges from the spine on a rough landing and rolls into the drain where it meets an unexplained muddy hand — genuine mystery without arbitrary withholding. The embroidered white magpie on blue cloth simultaneously proves identity, establishes the sibling bond, and weaponises a domestic memory against the master's lie. The three-beat gong structure (second → third) functions as a real-time clock that compresses every decision. Nothing decorative survives; nothing causal is missing.

**样本 02 — REJECTED.**

Three rejection-level defects:

1. **Broken causality / register breach.** The final image — rain soaking the blue cloth band in his shoe and producing "a thread of fresh blood" (一缕新鲜的血) — introduces a supernatural or symbolic event inside an otherwise realistic-physical register. No framework supports it. The blood is not the sister's (she is absent), not his (he is unwounded there), and not residual (it is specified as 新鲜). This is empty rhetoric dressed as a closing image.

2. **Convenience plotting.** The copper badge on the master's inner sleeve rotates into view at exactly the moment the disciple needs proof of complicity. The burned page happens to char in a ring that preserves precisely the half-line of actionable intelligence ("North Warehouse No. 7, tonight at midnight, switch people"). Both are author-serving, not character-driven.

3. **Anti-formula failure.** The beat sequence — bloody booklet → master orders burn → hidden textile with sister's mark → confrontation → escape through rear window → legible fragment → atmospheric closing — replicates the pressure-scenario template without introducing a single structural inversion. The disciple pressing a burning page against his own palm to force the master's release is the one inventive physical beat, but its causal mechanism is left vague (the master lets go because of 焦味, burnt smell — why would smell, rather than heat or pain to the disciple, break a grip?).

**样本 03 — No rejection, but flagged concerns.**

The secret passage from the dart bureau's back wall leading directly to Warehouse 7 is a significant convenience; no spatial or narrative logic explains why this tunnel exists. The medicine-cabinet-to-iron-basin transition is functional but arbitrary furniture. However, these do not reach rejection threshold because the final sequence — the bell answering from behind the door, the master's warning "what comes out won't be your sister," and the voice saying "don't light the lamp" — delivers genuine tonal disruption and reframes the entire preceding action as potentially insufficient preparation. The thin wooden slip (not paper) as hidden medium is a welcome material choice. The master's final line implies transformation rather than death, which is the strongest single relationship beat across all three samples.

## Routing Recommendation

- **样本 01**: Route to production pipeline. No revision needed. This sample demonstrates full command of physical causality under time compression, embodied sensory integration, and earned emotional stakes. The closing image (key against iron grate, muddy hand, extinguished lantern) is a three-part hook that operates on plot, mystery, and atmosphere simultaneously.

- **样本 02**: Do not route. The writer demonstrates baseline competence in scene-level choreography but cannot sustain causal logic through a closing image and defaults to formulaic beat sequencing. Recalibration on register discipline and anti-convenience plotting required before resubmission.

- **样本 03**: Route to revision queue. The writer's instinct for relationship tension and tonal subversion in the final beats is strong (the bell, the voice, the master's warning are all earned). The mid-section convenience plotting (direct tunnel, visible key ring) needs one structural pass to embed spatial logic. With revision, this sample is viable.

## Verdict

RECALIBRATE

The seven-year backstory (arrow, malaria) is deployed as evidence in 陆槐's indictment, not as expository ornament. **Sample 05 — REJECTED.** Grounds:

- **Structural copying / template duplication.** The skeleton is sample 04 with nouns exchanged: two figures in a remote natural chamber → rare medicine in a ritual vessel → hidden prescription dated three days out → wrist marks of terminal illness → struggle over who controls the medicine → environmental interruption as curtain. The correspondence is scene-beat exact, not merely thematic.

**Sample 06 — REJECTED.** Grounds:

- **Structural copying, aggravated.** Reproduces the same skeleton a second time. The prop list is now openly interchangeable with 04 and 05: white lines on wrist ≈ black lines on wrist; porcelain bottles ≈ jade box ≈ celadon cup; hidden prescription ≈ hidden prescription ≈ hidden prescription; fire tinder box hidden inside broken shovel handle ≈ letter hidden inside fire striker. Variation is cosmetic; architecture is identical.

There is no equivalent of 04's "这个为什么不记？" / "记了你就不会来" exchange, where the deception is indivisible from the relationship's specific emotional logic.
- **Unusable causality.** The numbered bottle cap ("三") rolling into an ice crack is inert symbolism. It produces no consequence and no decision. The clapper-less bell is a strong image orphaned from any causal chain — it signifies but does not act.
- **Anti-formula failure.** By the third iteration, the template is the formula. A reader encountering these in sequence would predict every beat after the medicine is found.

## Routing Recommendation

Sample 04 is publication-caliber prose with no structural defects. It should route forward without revision.

Samples 05 and 06 should not route at all. They are not weak drafts of independent scenes; they are re-skinned duplicates of 04's architecture. Revision cannot fix template dependence — only new invention can. If the writer is being evaluated for range, these two samples constitute negative evidence: they demonstrate that the considerable skill visible in 04 is currently locked to a single narrative chassis (remote discovery → hidden terminal diagnosis → medicine struggle → environmental interruption). The writer should be directed to produce samples with fundamentally different scene structures before re-evaluation.

## Verdict

**RECALIBRATE**

Sample 04 alone would merit ACCEPT without hesitation — it is precise, causally airtight, and emotionally irreducible. But a calibration set exists to test range, and two of three samples are structural clones. The writer possesses the craft; the writer has not yet demonstrated the architectural variety that distinguishes a reliable producer from a one-pattern specialist. Return to calibration with new samples required to use different scene shapes.

### Sample 07 — Notes

Causality is airtight and layered: the mother's warning ("never open the door for someone with a copper bell on the right wrist") is established inside the memory the boy must burn; saving the girl costs him the very knowledge that would have protected him from her. The rule's three conditions (seven breaths, a complete memory spoken aloud, release = mutual death) are introduced cleanly, then each beat of the countdown is used to dramatize the loss — never merely to clock tension. The scene is embodied at the level of texture: fingernails scoring four white marks on brick, honey-coloured light on the mother's cheek, the bone segments audibly slotting back through skin. The final image — the boy weeping on bleached stone steps without knowing why — earns its weight because the gap in his mind is the reader's fresh knowledge. The copper bell and the black door are not sequel bait; they are the immediate, causal detonation of the price he just paid. No element in this sample is decorative.

### Sample 08 — Notes

The architecture is legible: seven pages of sealed memory, burn one page to buy half a moment, lose that memory forever. The oil-lamp page and the mother combing hair carry real sensory warmth. However, this sample replicates Sample 07's skeleton with lower-resolution choices. The stranger-who-must-be-saved, the mother's posthumous artifact, the memory-as-fuel mechanic, the riders arriving to add time pressure, the posthumous maternal note that warns against the next step — all map onto 07's structure beat for beat, and arrive in the same order. The closing twist (a new page appearing with the mother's handwriting saying "don't go") has punch, but the umbrella-seller functions as an exposition courier with no interiority, and the three riders are threat décor rather than agents with discernible motive. The "black nail" locking system is stated but not dramatized with the same cause-and-effect rigour: the red thread retreating into the fire is visually neat yet mechanically unexplained within the sample's own rules. Prose is competent — the rain-sound receding when the page ignites is a good sensory beat — but the piece reads as a structural echo rather than an independent organism.

### Sample 09 — Notes

The three-sentence rule (lose voice-memory, lose face-memory, lose causal memory) is the strongest mechanical spine of the three samples, and its partial completion creates a genuinely unsettling outcome: the boy forgets *why* he is saving the man, so he lets go — not out of cowardice but out of the rule literally eating his motive. That is causality doing real narrative work. The plum-blossom seal migrating up the vein toward the heart is a fine closing image that converts the incomplete ritual into an ongoing bodily threat. The white jade token bearing the surname "Shen" plus the mother's seal introduces a kinship ambiguity without over-explaining it. Weaknesses: the rust obscuring the third line edges toward convenience plotting (the information is hidden because the plot needs it hidden, not because of an in-world cause), and the pale hands rising from the water are a genre-standard image that the sample doesn't defamiliarise. The man's dramatic reversal ("Don't say it! You'll forget her!") is well-timed but slightly neat — he shifts from desperate supplicant to selfless protector in a single line without intermediate resistance. Overall, strong work with minor mechanical seams.

## Rejection Reasons

**Sample 08 — REJECTED**

- **Structural copying signal.** The beat sequence (posthumous maternal artifact → sealed memories as consumable fuel → stranger in mortal danger → sacrifice one memory to save stranger → sensory flashback of lost memory → maternal posthumous warning against the next action) duplicates Sample 07's architecture in the same order, with parallel props (copper box / thin iron plate, seven pages / three-line inscription, arriving horsemen / collapsing roof). When two calibration samples share this degree of skeletal congruence, the later one cannot be evaluated as an independent piece.
- **Underdramatised rules.** The lock-soul nail's mechanics are asserted by the umbrella seller but never causally tested or complicated within the scene. The red thread's behaviour during the burn is visually described but not governed by any stated constraint, making it rhetorical rather than structural.
- **Flat secondary cast.** The umbrella seller exists only to deliver exposition and a prop; the three riders exist only to provide a deadline. Neither exerts causal force on the protagonist's decision.

Samples 07 and 09 — no rejection reasons identified.

## Routing Recommendation

- **Sample 07** → Route to production pipeline with no revision flag. The rule system, sensory embodiment, and ironic reversal are fully integrated. This sample can serve as a benchmark for the `rule_cost` archetype.
- **Sample 08** → Do not route. Return to the writer pool with a structural-originality flag and a note that the memory-fuel mechanic must be rebuilt from different narrative premises if re-attempted.
- **Sample 09** → Route to production pipeline with a minor-revision flag. Two items for revision attention: (a) provide an in-world cause for the third line's illegibility rather than relying on rust as narrative convenience; (b) consider giving the pale river-hands a single distinguishing detail to lift them above genre stock imagery.

## Verdict

**RECALIBRATE**

Rationale: Sample 07 is excellent and Sample 09 is strong with minor fixable seams, but Sample 08's structural duplication of 07 indicates the writer is generating variations on a single template rather than producing independent narrative organisms. The set as a whole demonstrates high craft ceiling but insufficient architectural range. Accepting all three would validate pattern-repetition; rejecting all three would discard genuinely accomplished work in 07 and 09. The correct action is to accept 07 and 09 conditionally, reject 08, and require the writer to produce a replacement sample that demonstrates the `rule_cost` archetype through a fundamentally different dramatic structure before full acceptance.
