### Literary Mechanism Review

#### 1. Core Novel DNA (`single_book_dna`)
*   **Linear Cultivation Hierarchy:** A strict, 12-tier progression system (from *Dou Zhi Qi* 斗之气 up to *Dou Di* 斗帝) that establishes clean, quantitative power milestones.
*   **Technique Evolution via Heavenly Flame Absorption:** The signature mechanic of the *Flame Mantra* (焚诀), which allows the protagonist to upgrade his cultivation technique grade by consuming rare and volatile *Heavenly Flames* (异火).
*   **Dual-Progression Archetype:** Protagonist functions concurrently as a martial fighter (*Dou Zhe*) and a utility crafter (*Alchemist* 炼药师), allowing him to build economic leverage and bypass cultivation bottlenecks.
*   **The Mentor-in-a-Ring Catalyst:** The trapped soul of a legendary master (*Yao Lao* 药老) who serves as a portable library, early-stage combat reservoir, and narrative guide.
*   **Geographically Anchored Conflicts:** A structure starting with a localized family rivalry and a broken engagement (*Three-Year Agreement* 三年之约) that escalates to continental and extra-dimensional proportions.

#### 2. Long-Form Narrative Mechanisms (`long_form_mechanisms`)
*   **Suppressed-Release Cycles (先抑后扬):** The primary engine of narrative tension. The protagonist faces underestimation, insults, or severe physical setbacks, followed by a dramatic breakthrough and public victory (*装逼打脸*).
*   **Geographic Map Shifting (地图切换):** Gradual expansion of the setting to correspond with power progression: Wu Tan City $\rightarrow$ Jia Ma Empire $\rightarrow$ Black-Corner Region/Jia Nan Academy $\rightarrow$ Central Plains (Zhong Zhou) $\rightarrow$ Ancient Space Realms.
*   **Alchemist Economy:** Pill-refining acts as a transactional currency, allowing the protagonist to recruit powerful protectors (e.g., *Dou Huang* and *Dou Zong* experts) far above his own weight class.
*   **Antagonist Escalation Scale:** Progression from local family rivals to national sects (*Misty Cloud Sect* 云岚宗), to a continent-spanning dark faction (*Hall of Souls* 魂殿), and finally the ancestral *Soul Clan* (魂族).

#### 3. Originality Risks & Genre Tropes (`originality_risks`)
*   **Formulaic Retaliation Loops:** Heavy reliance on repetitive cycles of arrogant antagonists challenging the protagonist, leading to predictable patterns of face-slapping.
*   **Late-Stage Power Inflation:** Rapid scaling of power tiers near the end makes lower-tier cultivation achievements and historical factions completely irrelevant.
*   **Companion Marginalization:** Side characters and potential love interests are periodically sidelined as the protagonist outgrows their geographic region and cultivation levels.
