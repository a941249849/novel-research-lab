bounded planning pass. The current evidence favors upgrade/cost/state-change
mechanisms and should stay at system-design level.

single book pattern as a template to copy.

The planning pass should abstract cost, backlash, scarcity, and social
recognition patterns before naming any specific technique.

- Require a cost or state change for every planned technique category.
- Keep evidence references attached to the plan.
