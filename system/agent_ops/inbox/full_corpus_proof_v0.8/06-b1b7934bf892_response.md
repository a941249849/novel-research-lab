### Literary Mechanism Review for 《圣墟》

#### 1. Single Book DNA
*   **Pollen Evolution Path (花粉进化路):** Unlike traditional cultivation setups utilizing ambient Qi, evolution in *Sheng Xu* relies on harvesting the pollen and fruits of ancient spiritual plants to force genetic mutations, injecting a post-apocalyptic, semi-scientific evolutionary flavor into the early narrative.
*   **Post-Apocalyptic Global Mutation (天地异变):** The story starts on a reformed Earth where mountain ranges expand, ancient mythological coordinates reappear, and local wildlife undergoes rapid genetic awakening.
*   **Trilogy Convergence (遮天三部曲终章):** As the final installment of Chen Dong's trilogy (*Perfect World* $\rightarrow$ *Shrouding the Heavens* $\rightarrow$ *Sheng Xu*), its structural narrative is built to connect and resolve the cosmic lore of Shi Hao (荒天帝) and Ye Fan (叶天帝).
*   **Reincarnation Loop Dynamics (轮回与小阴间):** The universe is structured into tiers (Netherworld/Small Underworld and the Yang Realm) separated by physical reincarnation paths, soul light energy, and magical reincarnation papers.
*   **Banter-Heavy Companion Archetypes:** The social core of the narrative is dominated by satirical and humorous interactions between the protagonist and eccentric animal companions (like a mute golden bull and a toxic toad).

#### 2. Long-Form Cultivation and Combat Mechanisms
*   **Genetic Shackle Breaking (枷锁境):** Cultivators break bodily shackles to unleash dormant genetic capabilities, providing clear milestone progression and distinct superhero-like mutations early on.
*   **Tiered Ascension Hierarchy:** The cultivation system moves through stages such as Awakening (觉醒), Shackle (枷锁), Extraordinary (逍遥), Form/Visualization (观想), 餐霞 (Haze Eating), 塑形 (Body Sculpting), Gold Body (金身), Sub-Sage (亚圣), Sage (圣者), Reflection of the Heavens (映照诸天), and higher Yang Realm realms (Hunyuan, Taiyi, etc.) culminating in the Sacrificial Path (祭道).
*   **Dual-Cheat Progression Engine:** The protagonist relies on an untraceable, ancient grey Stone Box (石盒) to hide from cosmic entities and three mysterious seeds that bloom under unique pollen conditions to leap across tiers.
*   **Geographical Domain Combat (场域法阵):** A tactical combat system where the protagonist bypasses raw power level deficits by manipulating geographic energy networks, runes, and crystal arrays.

#### 3. Originality and Structural Risks
*   **Severe Power Inflation:** The power scale escalates rapidly from regional combat to galaxy-destroying clashes, rendering early evolutionary systems and shackle-breaking concepts obsolete.
*   **Repetitive Face-Slap Trope Cycle:** The narrative relies heavily on the protagonist assuming aliases (e.g., Cao De) to hide his identity, getting underestimated by big-sect disciples, and systematically crushing them.
*   **Convoluted Ending Revisions:** The final war against the ominous source escalates too quickly, leading to mass character deaths and subsequent major revisions/extra chapters (番外) due to real-world pacing issues.
*   **Derivative Narrative Dependency:** The late-stage plot shifts from Chu Feng’s personal evolution to resolving the legacy of Ye Fan and Shi Hao, making the protagonist feel like a secondary helper in his own story.
