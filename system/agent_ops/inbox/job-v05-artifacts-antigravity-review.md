## Job ID

job-v05-artifacts-antigravity-review

## Structural Review

The artifacts creation asset family is currently in a seed state (readiness score: 20/100) and lacks models for item lifecycle, usage constraints, cost/backlash limits, upgrade paths, and a visual symbol ledger. The current artifacts.schema.json is deficient, restricted to basic envelope fields and payload keys ("cost" and "upgrade_stage" in the artifact object) without enforcing required properties. However, the source evidence pack provides sufficient structured DNA (18 research items, 10 cross-book mechanisms) to establish a formal rule framework. The review focuses on establishing long-form vitality by aligning all future artifacts with the core project theme of a heavenly debt-auditing system (《借天改命账》), translating generic fantasy treasures into tools of accounting, auditing, and debt regulation.

## Originality Risk

- Direct Noun and Concept Cloning: High risk of copying iconic objects from the training corpus, such as "掌天瓶" (Zhangtian Bottle), "天逆珠" (Tianni Bead), "万物母气鼎" (Mother Qi Tripod), "大黑伞" (Great Black Umbrella), "忘情水" (Forgetful Water), or "神秘石盒/三颗种子" (Mysterious Stone Box / Three Seeds).
- Cheat-Code Trivialization: Risk of implementing classic "cheat" items that grant passive, cost-free progression boosters, undermining the auditing/debt theme.
- Mitigation: All artifacts must be bound to the originality policy: they must not copy physical descriptions, names, or exact functions of the source models. Instead, they must be translated into auditing/accounting instruments (e.g., "残命灯盏", "改账朱笔", "天意算盘") where every benefit demands a tracked, measurable debt or backlash state in the ledger.
