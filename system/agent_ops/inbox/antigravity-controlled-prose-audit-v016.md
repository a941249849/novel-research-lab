# Antigravity Controlled Prose Audit v0.16

## Verdict

ACCEPT. The bounded Hermes scene test for `《借天改命账》` successfully satisfies all literary and structural criteria established in the asset bundle. The text displays high narrative quality and strictly adheres to the established physical and emotional constraints.

## Strengths

- **Vivid Physical Backlash**: The cost of casting `烬痕归火术` is rendered with visceral clarity (immediate growth of white hair at the temples, skin splitting to reveal grey-red light under the ribs, and coughing up cold ash that scorches the stone slabs), ensuring the magic system's physical toll is visually concrete.
- **Active Communal Agency**: The residents of Mudlamp Lane are depicted as active participants in their survival network. Aunt Zhou guarding the door with a hot shovel, the lame craftsman bracing the array with his bleeding shoulder, and the women pooling their lanterns to shelter the children demonstrate mutual aid rather than presenting the community as passive background fodder.
- **Sensory Magic Translation**: Abstract concepts of taxation, collection, and compensation are masterfully translated into sensory fantasy elements, such as grey ash rain that leaves dark red marks on the skin, and the cracked clay lamp resembling a dry riverbed containing silver-grey fire.
- **Emotional Integrity**: The maternal relationship functions as a strong emotional anchor. Su Wan's moral instruction not to drain the neighbors' life-fire restricts the protagonist's actions, while her remaining cracked lamp maintains active deadline pressure.
- **Elimination of Technical Jargon**: The text is entirely free of database, ledger, or system terminology (such as "ledger states" or "exposure rates"), expressing all mechanics through organic, in-world Chinese fantasy concepts.

## Blocking Issues

- **None**: The prose sample exhibits no blocking issues or rule violations.

## Revision Priorities
