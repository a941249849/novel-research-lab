## Structural Review

The techniques creation asset family is currently minimal (readiness score: 10/100) as the `techniques.schema.json` is restricted only to basic fields (`cost` and `counter_ids`) without enforcing systemic progression ladders, role-playing constraints, exposure risks, or group-level cost-sharing buffers. However, the source evidence pack provides sufficient structured DNA (18 research items, 7 cross-book mechanisms) to establish formal structural rules. The review focuses on establishing long-form vitality by aligning all technique functions and power dynamics with the core project theme of a heavenly debt-auditing system (《借天改命账》), transforming techniques from generic martial combat abilities into legalistic tools of ledger regulation, audit enforcement, and debt balancing—specifically "改账" (ledger modification) and "算劫" (tribulation calculation)—whose execution and progression are strictly driven by and recorded in state-ledger parameters.

## Originality Risk

- Direct Tropes Cloning: High risk of copying recognizable technique names, realms, or progression ladders from the training corpus, such as "焚诀" (Fenjue) and "异火" (Heavenly Flames) from *Doupo Cangqiong*, "大命运术" (Great Destiny Technique) from *Yongsheng*, "他化自自在法" (Hehua Zizai) from *Wanmei Shijie*, "扮演法" (Acting Method) from *詭秘之主*, or "春秋蝉" (Autumn Cicada) and "蛊虫" (Gu worms) from *Gu Zhenren*.
- Linear Progression Trivialization: Risk of implementing standard martial arts or cultivation tier systems (e.g., Qi Condensation to Qi Integration) that progress via simple numeric leveling, ignoring the thematic balance-sheet and debt-auditing rules of the setting.
- Mitigation: All techniques must be structurally bound to the ledger-auditing theme, representing them as "改账" (ledger-auditing) and "算劫" (calculating tribulation) methods. Technique activation and advancement must demand tracked, measurable debits or backlash states (e.g., `provincial_troop_loyalty`, `relic_dream_timer`) on the state ledger, and proper nouns from the source novels are strictly blacklisted.
