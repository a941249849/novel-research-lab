## Timeline

### 0. Original Research Lineage

Decision:

- Treat existing GitHub writing Agents, writing Skills, InkOS-like engineering thought, Story Bible systems, Critic Loops, multi-agent writing systems, two deep research reports, and the Chinese web-novel corpus as source inspiration.

Reason:

- Existing systems exposed the same core gap: one-shot prompting is not enough for long-form fiction. A durable system needs persistent world/state records, review loops, and controlled agent roles.

Impact:

- Novel Research Lab was framed as an industrial research-and-creation system, not as a prompt collection.

### 1. Phase 0C Original Vision Reconciliation

Decision:

- Reconcile the original "grand synthesis" ambition against the v1 MVA architecture.

Reason:

- The early implementation risked shrinking the system into a demo. Phase 0C preserved the full lineage: Story Bible, World Building, Tree of Outlines, State Machine, True/Public Ledger, Critic Loop, multi-agent orchestration.

Impact:

- v1 was explicitly defined as a minimum viable architecture, not the full final system.
- Missing capabilities were recorded instead of forgotten.

### 2. system_master_spec_v0.1

Decision:

- Approve `system_master_spec_v0.1.md` as the Phase 1A baseline.

Reason:

- The system needed a single engineering specification that stated hierarchy, tool roles, roadmap, and prohibitions.

- Hermes became the creative consumer/writer.

### 3. Phase 1A Engineering Toolchain

- Boundary-string hygiene was recorded as non-blocking.

### 7. Phase 2A-2R Grand Synthesis Reopening

Decision:

- Reopen creative space instead of forcing a three-way project selection.

Reason:

- The three candidates were valid but too narrow. They proved mechanism compliance, but did not yet embody the original "grand synthesis" ambition of a long-form web-novel engine.

Impact:

- Existing candidates became a concept material pool, not final project choices.
- Mechanism library was reclassified as fuel, not menu.
- Schema was reclassified as organizing tool, not creative cage.
- The anti-copy standard moved toward structure-level originality.

### 8. Current Owner Redefinition of "Grand Synthesis"

Decision:

- Record that "grand synthesis" means absorbing market-, time-, and reader-validated narrative vitality, not merely combining mechanism IDs.

Reason:

- The original system lineage included GitHub writing systems, InkOS / Skill engineering, Story Bible, outline trees, ledgers, Critic Loop, research reports, and the Chinese web-novel corpus. A mechanical candidate template cannot carry that full inheritance.

Impact:

- Next creative stage must open with world-scale vision, protagonist fate, long-term resource struggle, market/reader resonance, and then converge into BookDNA / NarrativeDNA / StateLedger.
- Future reviews must watch three failure modes: engineering process without creative life, mechanism index without market/time validation, and candidate templates without a grand synthesis engine.
