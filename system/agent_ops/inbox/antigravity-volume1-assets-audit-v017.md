# Antigravity Volume 1 Assets Audit v0.17

## Verdict
ACCEPT_WITH_WARNINGS. The thirteen proposed assets successfully address all structural coverage gaps identified in the v0.17 readiness audit, aligning with the Story Bible and the Volume 1 outline tree. The warnings concern potential state ledger synchronization redundancy between the newly added individual strain tracking and the existing Poverty City communal return fire ledger.

## Structural Coverage
The expansion pack provides comprehensive structural coverage by successfully materializing all thirteen assets required to promote Volume 1 from seed status to production readiness:
*   **NPCs**: Fully defined cards for Su Wan (character anchor), Qiu Shen (local bureaucratic antagonist), Gu Xun (action-phase collector antagonist), and Wen Zhaowei (prefecture shadow envoy).
*   **Factions**: Established structures for the Life Registry Office (Poverty City administrative jurisdiction) and the Prefecture Life Treasury (regional quota/transfer hierarchy).
*   **Techniques**: Formulated detailed mechanical costs and feedback for Ledger Recognition (investigative sensory tool) and Chain Deflection / Seal Breaking (defensive combat印).
*   **Artifacts**: Defined the Prefecture Transport Registry Seal as a limited regional transport evidence clue.
*   **Naming**: Implemented the Prefecture-Level Naming Style to control naming conventions for higher-level zones and entities.
*   **Emotional Bonds**: Modeled the Community Trust Bond to track the reciprocal mutual-aid dynamics of Mudlamp Lane.
*   **Foreshadowing**: Outlined the Karmic Ledger Heritage to anchor fragments of the ancient world-borrowing covenant.
*   **State Ledgers**: Integrated individual physical and cognitive strain via the protagonist strain ledger.

## Consistency and Originality
The expansion assets successfully implement the core world-building constraints:
*   **Mother Debt**: Preserved via Su Wan’s constraints. She is defined as an active agent with the power to refuse sacrifice, establishing a firm moral boundary rather than functioning as a passive plot device.
*   **Community Agency**: Upheld in the community trust bond, giving Mudlamp Lane residents the right to query, refuse, and withdraw witness statements.
*   **Embodied Cost**: Enforced in both techniques. Casting leads to visceral physical backlash (white hair growth, split skin, bone fractures, cold ash coughs, and memory loss) tracked directly on the protagonist strain ledger.
*   **Pacing Variation**: Maintained by boundary limits on the shadow envoy, prefecture treasury, and transport seal, preventing premature scale inflation and preserving local tension.
*   **No-Jargon**: Enforced across all asset constraints, preventing technical database terms from appearing in user-facing text and replacing them with visual equivalents (abacuses, ink-grey rain, and cracked lamps).
*   **Originality Red Lines**: Enforced in the prefecture naming convention, which explicitly bans standard cultivation clichés and training-set collisions (such as supreme bones, double pupils, or celestial abodes).

## Blocking Issues
No blocking narrative or structural defects exist. However, a significant consistency warning is noted:
*   **State Ledger Redundancy**: The newly introduced protagonist strain ledger tracks several variables (`caster_lifefire_stability`, `half_life_registry_exposure`, and `mother_life_lamp_cracks`) that are already tracked by the existing Poverty City return fire ledger. This metric overlap creates a synchronization risk during simulation and must be resolved by prioritizing the protagonist strain ledger for individual physical attributes and the return fire ledger for communal status.

## Production Readiness
With the integration of these thirteen assets, the volume's foundation is complete. Phase 1 production may proceed under a controlled framework, utilizing the approved prose test as the stylistic baseline. Initial scene generation must prioritize the physical backlash of techniques and the active participation of the Mudlamp Lane community.
